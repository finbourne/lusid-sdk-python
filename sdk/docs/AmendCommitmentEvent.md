# AmendCommitmentEvent

Amends the total committed capital of a CapitalInterest. A positive amount increases the commitment and a  negative amount decreases it.
## Properties
Name | Type | Description | Notes
------------ | ------------- | ------------- | -------------
**var_date** | **datetime** | The date the commitment amendment takes effect. | [optional] 
**amount** | **float** | The signed change to the total commitment. Positive implies an increase, and negative implies a  decrease. | 
**component** | **str** | The component of the commitment the amendment is recorded under: &#39;Commitment&#39; for a commitment made  or upsized, &#39;Release&#39; for committed capital released. Optional; when absent the sign of the amount  decides. | [optional]
